About

Free, welcoming and built by Arizona's security community

BSides Phoenix is an inclusive, community-run event that showcases Arizona's cybersecurity talent and the diverse people behind it. Come for the talks, stay for the workshops and villages, and leave knowing more of your neighbors in the field.

Every registration helps grow Arizona's nonprofit cybersecurity community and keeps BSides Phoenix free and welcoming for everyone.

FAQ

Good to know

Is BSides Phoenix really free?

Yes. General Admission is free. Supporter tickets ($50) are optional and help cover our operating costs.

Do I need a ticket if it's free?

Yes, everyone needs a ticket. We may not have extra tickets available at the door, so please register ahead of time.

Is lunch provided?

Lunch isn't provided. Plan to step out or grab food nearby during the break.

How does merch work?

Merch is ordered online and picked up at the conference; we don't ship. Orders close Sunday, September 12, 2027, and extras for day-of buyers aren't guaranteed.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. Everyone at BSides Phoenix agrees to our Code of Conduct.
