CFP

Call for papers

Have something to share? We're looking for talks and workshops from across Arizona's security community.

Key dates

  • December 12, 2026Call for papers opens, live at our virtual GHI Conference
  • June 30, 2027Call for papers closes
  • July 31, 2027Speakers notified
  • August 15, 2027Schedule published

How to submit

Submissions open December 12, 2026 and close June 30, 2027. Short talks and longer sessions are both welcome, and so are first-time speakers.

  • Short talk: 25 minutes
  • Session: 50 minutes, about 40 minutes of talk plus 10 for questions
  • Workshop: 1 hour 50 minutes, hands-on

Not sure which length fits? Choose "Either length" and we'll pick whichever works best in the program.

Get notified when it opens

For speakers

Speakers and workshop leads follow our Code of Conduct. If your material includes content that some people may find distressing, such as graphic images or discussion of abuse, add a content warning at the start and in your abstract.
