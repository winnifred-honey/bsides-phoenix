Volunteer at BSides Phoenix

BSides Phoenix is run by volunteers from Arizona's security community. Help make the day happen.

Key dates

  • July 1, 2027Volunteer sign-ups open
  • Week of October 4, 2027Volunteer training (planned)
  • October 16, 2027Conference day

What volunteers help with

  • The registration desk
  • Room monitors for talks and workshops
  • Other shifts across the day

Volunteers wear Bright Berry Pink VOLUNTEER shirts so attendees can find them.

A thank-you

Volunteers receive the event's limited edition enamel pin.
