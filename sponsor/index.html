Sponsor

Sponsor BSides Phoenix

Sponsors keep BSides Phoenix free for Arizona's security community.

Every sponsorship gives twice

Every sponsorship is paired with a local nonprofit, and part of it goes to them as a donation, so your support reaches further into Arizona's security community.

Who you'll reach

BSides Phoenix is a free, community-run conference for Arizona's cybersecurity community, from first-time students to longtime professionals. We're planning for 300 to 500 attendees over the day on Saturday, October 16, 2027, in Mesa, Arizona.

BSides Phoenix is organized by the Ginger Hacker Initiative, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Tiers and benefits

Our sponsor prospectus, with tiers and benefits, is planned for January 2027.
